Eridius Group

Eridius Group has ceased operations.

After extensive difficulties with our finances, staffing, and issues with upper management, Eridius's Primary Team Lead made the difficult decision to put Eridius Group LLC on hold for the long term. We have withdrawn from our fundraising efforts and are taking a much longer hiatus to get our ducks in a row. We do not know if or when we will return. Thank you to everyone who made Eridius worth something; it was an absolute honor.

Members of the Eridius Group team have been granted rights to, and copies of the source code for the projects they contributed to or inspired, and all artistic designs have been returned to their original authors. Works like Codychat Utilities and Sauron will continue on at the discretion of their original authors.

What was Eridius Group?

Eridius Group was a for-profit organization focused on raising public awareness about internet privacy and security. Too often, big tech companies use your private data—even in Incognito mode - to target you with ads, including harmful ones.

We believe this violation of user privacy is deeply unethical. The public deserves to know how to protect themselves from big tech's abuse of power.
